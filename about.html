Our Core Story

Redefining Student Living

Solving The Nigerian Student Housing Crisis

Every year, thousands of students at institutions like EBSU, UNN, UNILAG, and ABU struggle to find secure, affordable housing near their campuses.

We built StudentHome to cut out the aggressive agents, fake housing agents, and fraudulent offline listings. By acting directly as the digital bridge between trusted landlords and students, we save you time, money, and stress.

What started as a massive headache for new students has now transformed into a seamless tap-and-move-in experience.

🎯

Our Mission

To digitize and secure the campus housing infrastructure so every student can find a safe home near their university without being exploited by middlemen.

🤝

Our Vision

To become the singular, most trusted platform across all 36 states in Nigeria for landlords to verify their housing and for students to safely secure accommodations.

Why We Exist

The Old Way

  • Walking from street to street under the hot sun looking for "To Let" signs.
  • Paying ridiculous 50% "Agent Fees" and "Agreement Fees" to middlemen.
  • Falling victim to scammers posing as landlords on social media.
  • Renting a house only to discover it has no water supply or security.

With StudentHome

  • Browse hundreds of houses completely online from your phone.
  • Contact the actual landlord directly. Zero hidden agency fees.

  • Full transparency on amenities (water, security, distance to campus).
